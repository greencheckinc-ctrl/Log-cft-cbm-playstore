Delete your account and data

App: Log CFT/CBM Calculator by GreenCheck inc., India

How to request deletion

  1. Open the app and go to Settings > Activation. Note your Device ID.
  2. Send an email to greencheckinc@gmail.com from the email address you registered with.
  3. Write "Delete my data" in the subject and include your Device ID.

We will delete your request within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your login account (email address and Firebase Authentication record)
  • Your activation, plan and usage-count records
  • Any photo sent for AI reading (photos are not kept on our server after the reading)

What we may keep

Purchase and billing records that we must keep for legal and accounting reasons. Your calculator records are stored only on your own device; uninstalling the app or deleting files in the app removes them.

Deleting your data may stop your paid plan from working. Cancel your subscription in Google Play > Payments and subscriptions > Subscriptions.
