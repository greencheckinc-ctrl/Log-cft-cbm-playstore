Privacy Policy

Log CFT/CBM Calculator (“the App”) · Effective date: 4 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. The App is operated by GreenCheck inc., a proprietorship based in Shihori, Gujarat, India (“we”, “us”). Contact: greencheckinc@gmail.com.

In short: your measurement records stay on your device. We do not sell your data. Photos are sent to an AI service only when you tap “Read sizes”. AI readings are produced automatically and can be wrong, so you must check them (section 4).

2. How we use information

  • To provide and improve the App, including AI size reading.
  • To verify purchases, apply plans and prevent fraud or misuse.
  • To respond to support requests and send service notices (for example plan expiry).
  • To meet legal obligations.

We do not sell personal data and do not use your photos or records for advertising.

3. Who we share information with

We use trusted service providers who process data for us:

  • Google Firebase (Google LLC) – account login (Firebase Authentication) and our database for plans and activation.
  • Cloudflare, Inc. – runs our server that handles AI requests and purchase verification.
  • AI service provider (for example Anthropic, or Google Gemini / OpenAI where the App uses them) – receives the sheet photo (and zoomed crops of it) only when you tap “Read sizes”, to return the sizes. We do not intentionally store the photo on our server after reading. The provider processes it under its own terms and privacy policy, including its own data-retention practices. We do not control how long a provider may retain submitted content; please review the provider’s current privacy policy. AI providers may change their models at any time, so results can vary.
  • Google Play – payments, subscriptions and purchase verification.
  • Google AdMob – shows ads in the Free plan. AdMob may use the Android advertising ID and device information to show and measure ads, under Google’s own privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy). Premium removes ads. You can limit this in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights, users or the public. If the business is transferred, data may move to the new owner under this policy.

4. Photos and AI reading

Your consent. By buying an AI Support pack, you give your consent for the sheet photos you submit (and zoomed crops of them) to be sent to and processed by third-party AI service providers (see section 3) to read the sizes. You can withdraw this consent at any time by no longer using “Read sizes” or by emailing us; AI reading will then stop. Withdrawal does not affect processing already done, and refunds remain subject to our Terms.

Photos are sent only when you start a reading. The AI reading is produced automatically by a third-party AI model. It can misread handwriting, digits, rows or units, and can miss or add rows. You must check every size and quantity against the original sheet before using it. We are not responsible for any loss, wrong bill, wrong purchase or sale, dispute, payment or other decision that is caused by an AI reading that was wrong or was not checked by you. See also our Terms, section 4. AI extraction from images may contain errors even when the photo is clear. The App and its calculations are tools, not a professional, certified or legal measurement or advice, and we accept no professional responsibility for extracted or calculated values. Do not photograph sheets that contain information you are not allowed to share.

5. Backups and data loss

Your records are kept on your device. The App can save a backup file (Backup / Restore). Keep that file safe, because anyone who has it can read your records.

We do not keep a copy of your saved records. If your data is deleted or lost (for example you uninstall the App, clear its data, change or reset your phone, or the phone fails), we cannot get it back, and we are not responsible for the loss or for anything that follows from it. Please make regular backups.

6. How long we keep data

  • Records and photos on your device: until you delete them or uninstall the App.
  • Activation and plan records held by us: for as long as needed to provide the service, prevent misuse and meet legal and accounting needs.

Photos sent to an AI provider are also subject to that provider’s own retention policy. Google Play retains purchase records under Google’s policies; we do not control those records.

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal data, or to withdraw consent, by emailing greencheckinc@gmail.com with your Device ID (Settings > Activation). We will respond within a reasonable time, normally within 30 days. You can also delete files and photos inside the App. Deleting data may stop your plan from working. Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and other applicable Indian law, you have the right to access information about your personal data, to request correction and erasure of it, to withdraw your consent, to have your grievances addressed (see section 12), and to nominate another person to exercise these rights on your behalf if you die or become unable to do so.

Account and data deletion: email the address above from the email you registered with and write “Delete my data” with your Device ID. We will delete the account, activation and plan data that we control, except information we must keep by law. This request does not delete records stored locally on your phone, data held independently by Google Play or an AI provider, or your Google account. Delete local records separately in the App or on your device.

For complaints, see section 12 (Grievance contact).
