Privacy Policy

Log CFT/CBM Calculator (“the App”) · Effective date: 30 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. The App is operated by GreenCheck inc., India (“we”, “us”). Contact: greencheckinc@gmail.com.

In short: your measurement records stay on your device. We do not sell your data. Photos are sent to an AI service only when you tap “Read sizes”. AI readings are produced automatically and can be wrong, so you must check them (section 4).

2. How we use information

  • To provide and improve the App, including AI size reading.
  • To verify purchases, apply plans and prevent fraud or misuse.
  • To respond to support requests and send service notices (for example plan expiry).
  • To meet legal obligations.

We do not sell personal data and do not use your photos or records for advertising.

3. Who we share information with

We use trusted service providers who process data for us:

  • Google Firebase (Google LLC) – account login (Firebase Authentication) and our database for plans and activation.
  • Cloudflare, Inc. – runs our server that handles AI requests and purchase verification.
  • AI service provider (for example Anthropic, or Google Gemini / OpenAI where the App uses them) – receives the sheet photo (and zoomed crops of it) only when you tap “Read sizes”, to return the sizes. The photo is not stored on our server after reading. The provider processes it under its own terms and privacy policy. AI providers may change their models at any time, so results can vary.
  • Google Play – payments, subscriptions and purchase verification.
  • Google AdMob – shows ads in the Free plan. AdMob may use the Android advertising ID and device information to show and measure ads, under Google’s own privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy). Premium removes ads. You can limit this in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights, users or the public. If the business is transferred, data may move to the new owner under this policy.

4. Photos and AI reading

Photos are sent only when you start a reading. The AI reading is produced automatically by a third-party AI model. It can misread handwriting, digits, rows or units, and can miss or add rows. You must check every size and quantity against the original sheet before using it. We are not responsible for any loss, wrong bill, wrong purchase or sale, dispute, payment or other decision that is caused by an AI reading that was wrong or was not checked by you. See also our Terms, section 4. Do not photograph sheets that contain information you are not allowed to share.

5. Backups

Your records are kept on your device. The App can save a backup file (Backup / Restore). Keep that file safe, because anyone who has it can read your records.

6. How long we keep data

  • Records and photos on your device: until you delete them or uninstall the App.
  • Activation, plan and purchase records: for as long as needed to provide the service, prevent misuse and meet legal and accounting needs.

7. Your choices and rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal data, or to withdraw consent, by emailing greencheckinc@gmail.com with your Device ID (Settings > Activation). We will respond within a reasonable time, normally within 30 days. You can also delete files and photos inside the App. Deleting data may stop your plan from working. Rights under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and other applicable Indian law are respected.

Account and data deletion: email the address above from the email you registered with and write “Delete my data” with your Device ID. We will delete your account, activation and plan data, except what we must keep by law.

8. Security

We use reasonable measures such as encrypted connections (HTTPS), server-side protection of API keys and restricted write access to plan data. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children

The App is intended for business use by adults and is not directed to children under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

10. Data transfers

Our service providers may process data on servers outside India. By using the App you understand your data may be processed in other countries with appropriate safeguards from those providers.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The effective date above will change and, for important changes, we will tell you in the App. Continued use means you accept the updated policy.
